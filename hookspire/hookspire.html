        Capture the Flag

        Red vs Blue. Touch the other team's flag to grab it, then bring it to your base while your own flag is home. Stop a carrier while they're on your side of the map: touch them, or hook them and press W to yank. Once they cross the middle, they're safe. Touch your own dropped flag to send it home. First to 3 captures wins, with a 10 minute limit.

        Treasure Dive

        Dive into a cave full of treasure, grab what you can carry and bring it back up to your boat. Watch your air: bubble columns refill it, and so does the surface. Fish and eels knock treasure out of your hands, currents push you around, and the roof can cave in. Every dive is a new cave. Bank enough treasure to beat the level.

        Co-op

        Hook Racing

        Race 3 laps around a looping track. Pass every gate in order: the next one glows green, and a checkered line marks the finish. Fly through orange boost rings, find the narrow shortcuts in the middle wall, and grab ? boxes for items (press E to use one).

        Track

        Race type

        In a team race, every finishing place scores points for your team (10, 8, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1). The team with the most points wins.

        Bot difficulty

        Kingfall

        A real-time battle on a giant chess board. You have 8 pieces. Play as any of them and switch whenever you like, while the rest follow your orders. Take the enemy King to win.

        E attack · Q piece move · 1–8 or Tab switch piece. Click a piece's order in the list to give that piece a new one: Attack · Guard the King · Follow you · Hold where they are · Go to a spot you click · Attack the enemy King.

        Play as (vs bot)

        Online, the host picks who plays White and who plays Black in the lobby.

        Tokens: 2 for each enemy piece your side takes, 15 for winning.

              How to climb

              Mouse
              Aim the hook. The faint circle around you is its range.
              Space hold
              Fire and hold the hook. Let go to release and fly.
              W
              Reel in, pulling yourself toward the hook. On the ground, jump up one block.
              S
              Let rope out, up to the hook's range.
              A / D
              Swing left / right. Walk when standing.
              R
              Go back to your last checkpoint.
              Esc
              Pause.
              • Tokens. 1 each, plus 1 for every 10 m you climb on each climb, and 100 for the summit.
              • Hook rings. Always grabbable. From 180 m some slide.
              • Cracked rings (300 m) crumble a second after you grab them.
              • Stone ledges. Hook anywhere on them, or stand on them.
              • Steel blocks (60 m) can't be hooked and block your aim.
              • Spikes (120 m), saws (240 m), lasers (420 m) and moving saws (480 m) knock you off your rope.
              • Wind (360 m) pushes you sideways in gusts.
              • Checkpoint floors every 60 m. Pass up through them and land on top. The summit is at 780 m.
