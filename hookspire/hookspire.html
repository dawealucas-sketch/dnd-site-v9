How to climb

Mouse
Aim the hook. The faint circle around you is its range.
Space hold
Fire and hold the hook. Let go to release and fly.
W
Reel in, pulling yourself toward the hook.
S
Let rope out, up to the hook's range.
A / D
Swing left / right. Walk when standing.
R
Go back to your last checkpoint.
Esc
Pause.
  • Hook rings. Always grabbable. Higher up, some slide side to side.
  • Cracked rings crumble a second after you grab them, then re-form.
  • Stone ledges. Hook anywhere on them, or stand on them.
  • Steel blocks. Can't be hooked, block your path and your aim.
  • Spikes, saws and lasers knock you off your rope. Lasers flicker before they fire.
  • Wind shafts push you sideways in gusts.
  • Checkpoint floors every 60 m. You can pass up through them and land on top.

In co-op you can hook onto a teammate, too.
